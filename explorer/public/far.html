far protocol

Run a Far block

Named accounts, scoped keys, two shards, and a solver intent. CLAWD pays for authentication, attestation, verification, and inference, and that charge is the block producer's reward. Signatures are FIPS 204 ML-DSA-65. This page runs the in-memory protocol on the explorer. It does not spend Agave CLAWD, and it is not NEAR mainnet.

Starting a session…

Keys

A scoped key may ping app.clawd. Another account, key, or method leaves every balance unchanged.

Shards

One shard's fee and full queue stay on that shard. A receipt applies entirely or rolls back. Inclusion is one confirmation.

Intent

Alice signs the balance changes and a deadline, with no route. Settlement applies only when every asset nets to zero.

Mining

Valid included model work debits the payer and credits the producer. Unpaid, invalid, or failed work pays nothing.
